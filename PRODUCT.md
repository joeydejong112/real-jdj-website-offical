# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local business owners around Utrecht who need a professional website quickly, without a long agency process or unclear pricing. They are likely comparing practical options and want confidence that the provider is responsive, technically competent, and easy to work with.

## Product Purpose

JDJ Webdevelopment sells fixed website packages with fast delivery, clear scope, and direct WhatsApp contact. Success means visitors quickly understand the offer, trust the process, choose a package, and start a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Clear, fast, approachable. The page should feel professional enough for business buyers, but not cold or corporate.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS-template styling, decorative clutter, overproduced AI imagery, vague agency language, and animations that distract from the WhatsApp conversion path.

## Design Principles

1. Make the offer instantly legible: fixed packages, fixed price, fast online.
2. Keep the conversion path obvious: WhatsApp is the primary action.
3. Use friendly craft details sparingly so the page feels human, not gimmicky.
4. Preserve trust through restrained motion, readable type, and stable responsive layout.
5. Let package comparison stay simple and scannable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation and controls, meaningful alt text, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support for animation-heavy areas.
